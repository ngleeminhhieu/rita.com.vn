Our production process

A streamlined manufacturing journey designed for quality and scale — from raw material inspection to sealed, export-ready products, every step runs in a controlled and hygienic environment.

  1. 01

    Raw material inspection

    Only the finest ingredients pass our strict entry checks.

  2. 02

    Material transport to washing

    A controlled line keeps every ingredient clean and fresh.

  3. 03

    Raw material washing

    Multi-stage washing removes impurities and keeps the natural flavor.

  4. 04

    Changing area

    Hygiene gates prepare every team member for production.

  5. 05

    Preliminary processing room

    Ingredients are prepped to production-ready standards.

  6. 06

    Filter conveyor

    Continuous filtering protects purity at every transfer.

  7. 07

    Mixing & pasteurization

    Each recipe is blended to spec, then heat-treated for safety and shelf life.

  8. 08

    Filling & sealing

    Automated lines fill and seal every can and bottle in a closed, hygienic room.

  9. 09

    Incubation area

    Controlled incubation locks in quality and stability before export.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what buyers ask us most. Can’t find yours? Send it to our sales team and we will come back to you within one working day.

Contact us
How quickly will I hear back?

Our sales team usually replies within one working day (Vietnam time, GMT+7). For anything urgent, call our sales line on (+84) 274 3784 788 or email marketing@rita.com.vn.

What should I include in my inquiry?

The more we know, the faster we can quote: the product or flavor you have in mind, your target market, the packaging format and size, an estimated volume per order and whether it will carry your brand or RITA’s.

Can I get free samples first?

Yes. Samples are free and usually ready in 7–14 days, so you can check taste, quality and packaging before you commit. Tell us where they should go and we will arrange delivery.

What is the minimum order quantity?

RITA-branded products start from 200 cartons per order. Private label and custom formulas usually start from one 20ft container per SKU, and we can discuss a smaller first trial.

How long does an order take?

For products on an existing formula, goods are ready to ship 20–25 days after order confirmation. A new formula, new packaging or a market-specific certification brings it closer to 4–8 weeks.

Which payment terms and port do you use?

We work with L/C and T/T and ship from Ho Chi Minh Port, Vietnam. The Incoterm, such as FOB or CIF, is confirmed in your quotation.

Can I visit the factory?

Of course. Our head office and factory are in Song Than 2 Industrial Park, Di An Ward, Ho Chi Minh City. Share your preferred dates and we will plan the visit, from the production lines to quality control.
