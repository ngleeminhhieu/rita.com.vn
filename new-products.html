New products

The latest beverages from RITA’s R&D team — new flavors, new formats and ready-to-export recipes you can launch under your own brand or ours.

  • Fruit juice

    Orange carrot juice blend drink vitamin C 1L

    A bright, breakfast-ready citrus and carrot blend designed for effortless pouring, reliable flavor and strong shelf appeal — juicy orange lift with a smooth carrot finish for family tables, hotel buffets, cafés and workplace canteens.

  • Coconut products

    Canned coconut water matcha for energy

    RITA Coco 100% coconut water matcha flavor 500ml can blends the crisp sweetness of coconut water with the earthy smoothness of Japanese matcha — a light, rejuvenating tropical–Asian fusion.

  • Milk drinks

    11.1 fl oz cans protein milk shake orange flavor

    A creamy, refreshing and protein-rich milk shake crafted for today’s global market, pairing a fruity citrus taste with a smooth milk texture — indulgence and function in one can.

  • Sparkling drinks

    Sparkling rose apple drink 250ml cans

    RITA sparkling rose apple flavor 250ml sleek can — a tropical fruit delight with the crisp, floral sweetness of rose apple, part of a six-flavor sparkling range.

  • Sparkling drinks

    Sparkling peach kombucha tea drink with prebiotic

    The sweet and fizzy taste of RITA sparkling kombucha peach flavor with prebiotic in a 320ml can — a refreshing sparkling drink crafted for modern lifestyles and ready for global export.

  • Coffee drinks

    Iced Americano coffee with passion fruit 315ml sleek can

    A ready-to-drink cold brew that captures the strength of authentic Vietnamese coffee and the vibrant, exotic tang of passion fruit — a flavor journey in a 315ml sleek can.

  • Special drinks

    Bici Bici lychee juice with nata de coco 320ml PET bottle

    Delicate, exotic and refreshing — RITA Bici Bici lychee juice captures the sweet floral notes of lychee and adds chewy, fun nata de coco in every sip.

  • Tea drinks

    16.9 fl oz blueberry mint natural brewed tea

    A perfect blend of fruity boldness and minty freshness — RITA blueberry and mint tea in a 500ml glass bottle brings a new level of excitement to herbal tea drinks.
